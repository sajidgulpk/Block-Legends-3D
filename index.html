BLOCK LEGENDS 3D
Official Privacy Policy
Developer: SAJID AI LAB  |  Effective Date: October 25, 2026  |  Version: 1.0.0
Summary: Block Legends 3D is a 100% offline 3D block puzzle game developed by SAJID AI LAB. We do NOT collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data, location information, device identifiers, or user telemetry. All game progress and settings remain strictly on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how SAJID AI LAB ("we", "us", or "our") manages user privacy for the Android mobile application Block Legends 3D ("App"). We respect your personal privacy and are fully committed to protecting it through complete transparency.

2. Data Collection & Usage

Block Legends 3D does not collect any personal information or sensitive user data.

  • No Account Registration: You do not need an account, email, or sign-in to play.
  • No Location Access: We do not track or access your location or GPS data.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not capture names, contact details, device IDs, or IP addresses.
  • Local Game Storage: High scores, game modes, combo streaks, sound settings, and puzzle progress are stored locally on your device using Android Room Database and SharedPreferences.

3. Advertisements & Third-Party Analytics

The current version of Block Legends 3D contains NO advertisements, NO ad-tracking SDKs, and NO third-party analytics trackers. You can enjoy an uninterrupted offline 3D gaming experience without behavioral tracking.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Block Legends 3D is designed as a family-friendly and child-friendly 3D puzzle game suitable for players of all ages.

  • We comply strictly with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
  • We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EU).

5. Android System Permissions

Block Legends 3D requests only essential standard Android permissions necessary for local gameplay and feedback:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used exclusively to provide haptic feedback during 3D block placements and combo clears.

No internet permissions, camera access, microphone access, storage access, or contacts permissions are requested or required.

6. Security of Local Data

Because all data remains strictly local on your device, your information is protected by Android's built-in application sandboxing architecture. Clearing app data or uninstalling the game will erase local high scores and saved game progress.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect future features or regulatory updates. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
